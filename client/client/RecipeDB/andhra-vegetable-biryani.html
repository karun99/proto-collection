Andhra Vegetable Biryani

Royal Mutton Hub Kitchen
⏰ Prep: 20 min⏰ Cook: 30 min🍴 4 servings🟢 Easy💰 Low Budget💰 ₹120-160
🌸 gluten friendly

🛒 Ingredients

  • 1.5 cups basmati rice
  • 1 cup mixed vegetables (carrot, beans, peas, potato)
  • 1 onion (sliced)
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 tsp biryani masala
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric
  • 1/2 tsp red chilli powder
  • 2 tbsp ghee
  • 1/2 cup yogurt
  • Fresh mint & coriander
  • Salt to taste
  • Saffron soaked in 2 tbsp warm milk

📝 Instructions

  1. Wash and soak rice for 20 mins. Cook until 70% done and drain.
  2. Heat ghee, fry sliced onions until golden. Remove half for garnish.
  3. Add ginger-garlic paste and saute for 1 min. Add mixed vegetables and cook for 3-4 mins.
  4. Add yogurt, biryani masala, turmeric, chilli powder and salt. Cook for 2 mins.
  5. Layer rice over vegetables. Sprinkle saffron milk, mint and fried onions.
  6. Cover and cook on low flame for 15-20 mins.
  7. Mix gently and serve with raita.
💡 Tip: Use whatever vegetables are in season. Add a spoon of ghee on top before serving for extra flavour.

🔧 Kitchen Equipment Needed

⚙ Pressure cooker⚙ Pan⚙ Pot with lid
