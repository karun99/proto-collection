Classic Mutton Biryani

Royal Mutton Hub Kitchen
⏰ Prep: 30 min⏰ Cook: 1 hr🍴 4 servings🟡 Medium💰 Medium Budget💰 ₹350-400

🛒 Ingredients

  • 500g mutton (bone-in curry cut)
  • 2 cups basmati rice
  • 2 onions (sliced)
  • 2 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 cup yogurt
  • 2 tsp biryani masala
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp red chilli powder
  • 4 tbsp ghee
  • Saffron soaked in 2 tbsp warm milk
  • Fresh coriander & mint leaves
  • Salt to taste

📝 Instructions

  1. Marinate mutton with yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, biryani masala, turmeric, chilli powder and salt for 2 hours.
  2. Parboil rice until 70% done. Drain and set aside.
  3. Fry sliced onions in ghee until golden brown. Remove half for garnish.
  4. Add marinated mutton to remaining ghee and cook on high for 5 mins, then simmer covered for 20 mins.
  5. Layer parboiled rice over the mutton. Sprinkle saffron milk, coriander, mint and fried onions.
  6. Seal the pot with dough or foil. Cook on low flame for 25-30 mins.
  7. Let it rest for 5 mins, then serve with raita or salan.
💡 Tip: Use aged basmati rice for best results. The marination time is key for tender meat.

🔧 Kitchen Equipment Needed

⚙ Pressure cooker⚙ Heavy bottom pan⚙ Pot with tight lid⚙ Mixing bowl
