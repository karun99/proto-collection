Mutton Kebab (Seekh Kebab)

Royal Mutton Hub Kitchen
⏰ Prep: 40 min⏰ Cook: 20 min🍴 4 servings🟡 Medium💰 Medium Budget💰 ₹300-350
🌸 gluten friendly

🛒 Ingredients

  • 500g mutton keema (minced, ideally twice-ground)
  • 1 onion (finely chopped)
  • 2 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 2 green chillies (finely chopped)
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • 1 tsp cumin powder
  • 1/2 tsp chaat masala
  • 2 tbsp roasted gram flour (besan)
  • 2 tbsp fresh coriander
  • 1 egg
  • Salt to taste
  • Melted butter for basting

📝 Instructions

  1. Mix keema with all ingredients except butter. Knead well for 5-7 mins.
  2. Refrigerate the mixture for 30 mins.
  3. Soak skewers in water for 20 mins (if using wooden ones).
  4. Divide mixture into equal portions and shape around skewers in a cylindrical form.
  5. Grill on a gas grill or bake at 200°C for 15-18 mins, turning occasionally.
  6. Baste with melted butter and grill for 2 more mins.
  7. Serve hot with mint chutney and onion rings.
💡 Tip: Twice-ground keema gives smoother texture. Adding a bit of raw papaya paste helps tenderise.

🔧 Kitchen Equipment Needed

⚙ Grill or oven⚙ Skewers⚙ Mixing bowl⚙ Refrigerator⚙ Mincer
