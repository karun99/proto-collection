Mutton Nihari

Chef Ranveer Brar
⏰ Prep: 30 min + overnight⏰ Cook: 2 hr 30 min🍴 6 servings🔴 Hard💰 Medium Budget💰 ₹400-500
🌸 gluten friendly🥞 dairy friendly

🛒 Ingredients

  • 1 kg mutton shanks (nalli) or shoulder, large pieces
  • 1/2 cup ghee or oil
  • 3 tbsp whole wheat flour (for sheermal)
  • 2 tbsp ginger paste
  • 2 tbsp garlic paste
  • 1 onion, finely sliced (for garnish)
  • 1 tbsp ghee (for garnish)
  • Salt to taste
  • For spice bag (potli): 4 black cardamoms, 2 cinnamon sticks, 8 cloves, 1 tbsp fennel seeds, 1 tsp black peppercorns, 1 tsp cumin seeds, 1 mace (javitri), 2 bay leaves, 1 star anise — tie in muslin cloth
  • For Nihari masala: 2 tbsp fennel powder, 1 tbsp coriander powder, 1 tsp red chilli powder, 1 tsp turmeric, 1 tsp dry ginger powder, 1 tsp garam masala
  • For garnish: fresh ginger (julienned), fresh coriander, lemon wedges, green chillies (slit)

📝 Instructions

  1. Heat ghee in a heavy bottom pot (handi) on medium flame.
  2. Add mutton pieces and sear until golden brown on all sides. Remove and set aside.
  3. In the same pot, add ginger and garlic paste. Sauté for 2 mins until fragrant.
  4. Add all the Nihari masala powders (fennel, coriander, chilli, turmeric, ginger, garam masala). Cook for 1 min.
  5. Return the seared mutton to the pot. Add 6 cups of warm water and salt. Bring to a boil.
  6. Drop in the spice bag (potli). Reduce heat to low, cover and simmer for 2 hours. Stir occasionally.
  7. After 2 hours, remove the spice bag. The gravy should be thick and oil should separate.
  8. Mix whole wheat flour with 1/4 cup water to make a smooth slurry. Add to the gravy and stir continuously until it thickens (3-5 mins).
  9. For garnish: fry sliced onions in ghee until golden brown. Also fry ginger juliennes.
  10. Serve Nihari hot in bowls topped with fried onions, ginger, fresh coriander, lemon wedges and green chillies. Traditionally eaten with sheermal or khameeri roti.
💡 Tip: Nihari is all about slow cooking — the longer it simmers, the better it gets. Traditionally cooked overnight. The spice bag (potli) infuses flavour without leaving whole spices in the gravy. Nihari tastes even better the next day as flavours deepen.

🔧 Kitchen Equipment Needed

⚙ Heavy bottom pot (handi)⚙ Frying pan⚙ Spice grinder⚙ Muslin cloth (for spice bag)
