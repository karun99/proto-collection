Mutton Rogan Josh

Chef Sanjeev Kapoor
⏰ Prep: 25 min⏰ Cook: 1 hr 15 min🍴 4 servings🟡 Medium💰 Medium Budget💰 ₹350-450
🌸 gluten friendly🥞 dairy friendly

🛒 Ingredients

  • 1 kg mutton (shoulder or leg, bone-in), cut into pieces
  • 3 tbsp ghee or mustard oil
  • 2 large onions, thinly sliced
  • 1 cup curd (yogurt), whisked
  • 2 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 2 tsp fennel powder (saunf)
  • 1 tsp dry ginger powder (saunth)
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • 1 tsp red chilli powder (Kashmiri)
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric
  • 4 green cardamoms, crushed
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 2 cinnamon sticks
  • 4 cloves
  • 1/4 tsp asafoetida (hing)
  • 1 tbsp rose water (optional)
  • Salt to taste
  • Fresh coriander for garnish

📝 Instructions

  1. Heat 2 tbsp ghee in a heavy pan. Fry sliced onions until deep golden brown. Remove and grind to a fine paste.
  2. In the same pan, add remaining ghee. Add bay leaves, cinnamon, cardamom, cloves and asafoetida. Sauté for 30 seconds.
  3. Add mutton pieces and sear on high heat until browned on all sides (about 5-7 mins).
  4. Add ginger-garlic paste and cook for 2 mins until raw smell disappears.
  5. Add onion paste, whisked curd, fennel powder, dry ginger powder, chilli powder, turmeric and salt. Mix well.
  6. Cook on medium heat for 5 mins, stirring constantly. Add 2 cups warm water.
  7. Transfer to pressure cooker and cook for 4 whistles or until mutton is tender.
  8. Once done, add garam masala and rose water. Simmer for 5 mins uncovered.
  9. Garnish with fresh coriander. Serve with naan, roti or steamed rice.
💡 Tip: Kashmiri red chilli gives colour without too much heat. For authentic flavour, use mustard oil and let it smoke before cooking. Always use bone-in mutton for Rogan Josh — the marrow adds richness.

⚙ Heavy bottomed pan⚙ Pressure cooker⚙ Mixing bowls⚙ Knife set
