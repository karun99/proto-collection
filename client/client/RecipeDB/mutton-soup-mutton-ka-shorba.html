Mutton Soup (Mutton Ka Shorba)

Royal Mutton Hub Kitchen
⏰ Prep: 15 min⏰ Cook: 1 hr 30 min🍴 4 servings🟢 Easy💰 Low Budget💰 ₹100-150
🌸 gluten friendly🥞 dairy friendly

🛒 Ingredients

  • 500g mutton soup bones
  • 1 onion (chopped)
  • 2 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 2 black cardamom
  • 4 black peppercorns
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tbsp ghee
  • Fresh coriander & lemon wedges
  • Salt to taste

📝 Instructions

  1. Wash soup bones thoroughly.
  2. Heat ghee in a pressure cooker. Add cardamom, peppercorns, bay leaves and cinnamon.
  3. Add chopped onion and ginger-garlic paste. Saute until fragrant.
  4. Add soup bones and turmeric. Saute for 3-4 mins.
  5. Add 6 cups of water and salt. Pressure cook for 30-35 mins (6-7 whistles).
  6. Let pressure release naturally. Strain the broth.
  7. Shred any meat from bones and add back to soup. Garnish with coriander and serve hot with lemon wedges.
💡 Tip: For richer flavour, roast the bones before pressure cooking. The longer it simmers, the better the taste.
