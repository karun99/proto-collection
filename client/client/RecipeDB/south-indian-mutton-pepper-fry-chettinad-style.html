South Indian Mutton Pepper Fry (Chettinad Style)

Hebbar\
⏰ Prep: 25 min⏰ Cook: 35 min🍴 4 servings🟢 Easy💰 Medium Budget💰 ₹300-350
🌸 gluten friendly🥞 dairy friendly

🛒 Ingredients

  • 500g mutton (boneless, cubed small)
  • 3 tbsp black peppercorns (coarsely crushed) — adjust to taste
  • 2 tbsp fennel seeds (saunf)
  • 1 tbsp cumin seeds
  • 3 onions (1 finely chopped, 2 sliced)
  • 2 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 10 curry leaves
  • 2 sprigs fresh curry leaves for garnish
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric
  • 2 tbsp oil (preferably coconut oil for authentic flavour)
  • 1/2 cup grated coconut (optional, for Chettinad style)
  • Fresh coriander for garnish
  • Salt to taste

📝 Instructions

  1. Dry roast peppercorns, fennel seeds and cumin seeds until fragrant. Let cool and grind to a coarse powder.
  2. Pressure cook mutton with turmeric, salt and very little water (1/2 cup) for 2 whistles. Drain well.
  3. Heat oil in a heavy bottom pan (use coconut oil for authentic Chettinad flavour).
  4. Add curry leaves and chopped onion. Fry until translucent.
  5. Add ginger-garlic paste and cook for 2 mins until raw smell goes.
  6. Add the cooked mutton and stir-fry on high heat for 5-6 mins until slightly browned and dry.
  7. Add the freshly ground spice powder and salt. Reduce heat to medium. Mix well so every piece is coated.
  8. Continue stir-frying for 10-12 mins. The mutton should be dry and crispy on the edges.
  9. If using coconut, add it in the last 2 mins and roast until lightly golden.
  10. Turn off heat. Add sliced onions (for crunch), squeeze of lemon and toss once. Garnish with curry leaves and coriander. Serve as starter or with biryani.
💡 Tip: The key is freshly ground pepper — pre-ground pepper will not give the same punch. Coconut oil elevates the Chettinad flavour. For extra crunch, add sliced onions at the end without cooking — they should remain crisp and fresh.

🔧 Kitchen Equipment Needed

⚙ Pressure cooker⚙ Heavy bottom pan (kadai)⚙ Spice grinder⚙ Mixing bowl
